# Swiggy Power BI Dashboard

An interactive Power BI dashboard analyzing Swiggy restaurant data to uncover insights into restaurant performance, customer ratings, delivery time, pricing, cuisines, and city-level trends.

## 📊 Dashboard Overview

The dashboard provides insights into:

- Total number of restaurants
- Average delivery time
- Average restaurant pricing
- Total customer ratings
- Restaurant distribution by city
- Average ratings by cuisine
- Average delivery time by city
- Restaurant count by city
- Average price by cuisine
- Top cuisines based on total ratings
- Top-rated restaurants

## 🔍 Key Insights

- The analysis includes **5,195 restaurants** across multiple cities.
- The overall average delivery time is approximately **53.35 minutes**.
- **Kolkata** has the highest average delivery time at approximately **69 minutes**.
- **Surat** has the lowest average delivery time among the displayed cities at approximately **47 minutes**.
- **Chinese cuisine** receives the highest volume of customer ratings.
- Several cuisines maintain strong average customer ratings, indicating positive customer satisfaction.
- Pricing varies across cuisines, with some international cuisines positioned at higher average price levels.

## 💡 Business Recommendations

- Improve delivery efficiency in cities with higher average delivery times, particularly Kolkata.
- Promote high-demand cuisines such as Chinese through targeted campaigns and personalized recommendations.
- Increase visibility for highly rated restaurants and cuisines using featured placements or recommendation systems.
- Use pricing segmentation to target both premium and price-sensitive customer groups.
- Analyze operational factors such as restaurant preparation time, delivery distance, and delivery partner availability to further understand delivery delays.
